# Stop Paying for the Wait campaign plan

**Status:** Proposed  
**Market:** United Kingdom first  
**Primary conversion:** Qualified iOS activation  
**Secondary conversion:** Android waitlist signup  
**Launch shape:** Six-week campaign followed by a 90-day evidence programme  
**Campaign line:** **Stop paying for the wait.**

## 1. Campaign brief

Weekly releases can keep a viewer paying while a season slowly becomes available. Seasons can turn the release gap into a subscription gap by planning around finale dates, other wanted titles, existing subscriptions, and real billing periods.

The campaign must sell this mechanism before it sells an aggregate savings number. The proof should be transparent and reproducible:

> **10 weekly episodes. 3 billing dates. 1 month to binge.**  
> In this worked example, waiting until the finale could avoid two £9.99 renewals: **£19.98**.

The dated methodology and evidence are in [the streaming-savings marketing model](../../../SeasonsBackend/docs/research/streaming-savings-marketing-model-2026-08-23.md).

### Assumptions

- The first launch is lean and founder-led, with organic distribution before paid acquisition.
- The UK is the first market because the current proof and prices are UK-specific.
- The App Store is the active public conversion destination. Android remains a nurture audience until its public store availability is verified.
- The campaign describes a safe provider handoff. It never implies that Seasons cancels a third-party subscription.
- A projected saving and an observed outcome are different claims and remain visibly labelled.

## 2. Objectives and non-goals

### Campaign objectives

1. Make the timing problem instantly understandable.
2. Increase qualified users who install Seasons and create a current, complete Personal Plan.
3. Prove which message produces activation, not merely clicks.
4. Establish prospective evidence that can support a future observed savings claim.
5. Create a repeatable content engine around current finale dates and subscription timing.

### Non-goals

- Do not claim that an average user saves a fixed monthly or annual amount without representative evidence.
- Do not annualise one worked example.
- Do not describe a recommendation, provider handoff, or cancellation confirmation as money already saved.
- Do not depend on unpublished Provider Action guide coverage for launch.
- Do not use provider logos, show artwork, clips, music, or publicity stills without confirmed usage rights.

## 3. Audience

### Primary: the weekly-release stacker

A UK iPhone viewer paying for three or more services, following at least one weekly show, comfortable with bingeing, and frustrated by subscription creep.

**Job to be done:** “Tell me when I can stop paying without missing what I care about.”

### Secondary: the finale waiter

A fan searching for a specific show’s schedule, finale date, or best time to subscribe. This audience supplies the strongest current-show acquisition hooks.

**Job to be done:** “Show me the cheapest sensible time to watch this season.”

### Tertiary: the household planner

A budget-conscious household coordinating several wishlists and services. This is valuable longer-term, but should not lead until household planning and evidence are equally mature.

### Android nurture audience

Capture permission and preferred shows or services, then send useful finale-timing content while public availability is pending. Do not present the waitlist as an install path.

## 4. Message system

### Message hierarchy

1. **Problem:** You are paying while episodes build up.
2. **Promise:** Seasons shows when to watch and when a service is no longer needed.
3. **Mechanism:** It plans around release dates, your other shows, billing periods, and current subscriptions.
4. **Proof:** A dated, sourced worked example with visible assumptions.
5. **Action:** Build my savings plan.

### Core copy

**Hero**

> **Stop paying for the wait.**  
> Weekly releases can keep a streaming subscription running for months. Seasons helps you watch more in fewer paid months.

**Primary CTA**

> Build my savings plan

**Proof module**

> **10 weekly episodes. 3 billing dates. 1 month to binge.**  
> Waiting for the finale could avoid two £9.99 renewals in this Apple TV example: **£19.98**.

**Required qualification**

> Example assumes a UK £9.99 monthly plan, subscription starting 3 April 2026, monthly renewal on the 3rd, watching after the 5 June finale, and no other Apple TV viewing during the release window. Prices and dates can change.

### Claim ladder

| Stage | Permitted wording | Evidence required |
|---|---|---|
| Mechanism | “Seasons helps you time subscriptions around the shows you want.” | Verified product behaviour |
| Worked example | “In this example, two £9.99 renewals could be avoided.” | Dated schedule, price, assumptions, reproducible calculation |
| Personal projection | “Your plan could help you avoid £X over the next 12 months.” | Complete plan, exact tier, current price, eligibility rules, merged provider windows |
| Matured personal outcome | “You confirmed you were not charged on these expected renewal dates. Estimated saving: £X.” | Effective cancellation, matured billing dates, user or financial evidence, restart costs |
| Public cohort claim | “Among eligible users, the median estimated saving over 90 days was £X.” | Predefined cohort, zero outcomes included, reproducible analysis, advertising review |
| Annual claim | “Eligible users saved a median £X over 12 months.” | Full 12-month prospective cohort and independently reproducible evidence |

The words “up to” are not a substitute for substantiation.

## 5. Funnel

```text
Current-show hook
  -> sourced proof or calculator
  -> campaign landing page
  -> App Store visit or Android signup
  -> first open and account
  -> titles plus subscription portfolio
  -> current, complete Personal Plan
  -> recommendation viewed
  -> safe provider handoff
  -> external completion confirmed
  -> expected renewal dates mature
  -> outcome reported
  -> shareable estimated-savings proof
```

The first meaningful campaign conversion is a current, complete Personal Plan within seven days of an attributed first open. Landing clicks are a diagnostic, not the success definition.

## 6. Channel plan

### 6.1 Savings calculator and campaign page

**Idea:** Engineering and calculator marketing. Build a useful proof surface that calculates renewal charges from exact dates instead of presenting a generic average.

**Why it fits:** The calculation is the campaign’s proof, creates search demand, and naturally leads to the Personal Plan.

**Start:**

1. Publish `/stop-paying-for-the-wait/` with three sourced examples and the methodology.
2. Add a simple calculator using episode release dates, assumed billing date, exact tier price, and binge duration.
3. Offer App Store conversion after the result, plus general and Android-specific email capture.

**Expected outcome:** Higher-intent App Store traffic, organic links, and a reusable destination for every campaign asset.

**Resources:** One product marketer or founder, one web engineer, one designer, analytics support, and evidence review.

### 6.2 Short-form proof content

**Idea:** TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts built from one 15 to 25 second master format.

**Why it fits:** “10 episodes, 3 bills, 1 binge” is visual, surprising, and explainable without copyrighted footage.

**Start:**

1. Create four reusable templates: episode dots, billing calendar, two-strategy comparison, and personalised plan reveal.
2. Record three pieces in one weekly 90-minute batch.
3. Recut each master for vertical video, carousel, static post, email, and community discussion.

**Expected outcome:** Repeatable awareness, creative learning, saves and shares, and traffic to the proof page.

**Resources:** Founder-presenter or voiceover, designer/editor, one batch session per week.

### 6.3 Community and creator seeding

**Idea:** Value-first Reddit participation and 5 to 10 small TV, cord-cutting, and personal-finance creators.

**Why it fits:** The calculation is useful without requiring an immediate app pitch. Small creators can make the mechanism concrete for their own audience.

**Start:**

1. Publish the calculation template and ask communities where its assumptions fail.
2. Give each creator a sourced example relevant to their audience and a tracked landing link.
3. Invite remixes and corrections rather than prescribing an endorsement script.

**Expected outcome:** Trust, customer language, referral traffic, and stronger objections for landing-page copy.

**Resources:** Founder community time, evidence researcher, creator access or a small seeding budget.

### 6.4 Owned email and finale digest

**Idea:** A founder welcome sequence plus a weekly “Finales worth waiting for” digest.

**Why it fits:** Finale timing is recurring utility, not a one-off launch announcement. It also gives Android prospects value before release.

**Start:**

1. Add a general email capture separate from the Android waitlist.
2. Send a three-email welcome sequence: the problem, one worked example, then build a plan.
3. Start a weekly digest with sourced dates, checked prices, and one CTA.

**Expected outcome:** Repeat visits, higher activation, Android nurture, and a durable owned audience.

**Resources:** Founder or lifecycle writer, HubSpot setup, weekly evidence review.

### 6.5 App Store optimisation

**Idea:** Align the product page with the campaign promise.

**Why it fits:** A landing visitor should not encounter a different story after clicking through.

**Start:**

1. Make screenshot one communicate “Stop paying for the wait.”
2. Use the next screenshots to show add shows, see timing, and act on the plan.
3. Use campaign-specific store attribution where available and verify the live product-page result.

**Expected outcome:** Less message loss between campaign click and install.

**Resources:** ASO owner, app screenshot production, App Store Connect access.

### Paid acquisition gate

Do not buy broad reach initially. After landing attribution and activation events reconcile, test £10 to £20 per day for 14 days on the best organic creative through Apple Search Ads or tightly scoped retargeting. Scale only when qualified activation and retention are acceptable, not merely when clicks are cheap.

## 9. Measurement contract

### Primary metrics

1. **Qualified Plan Activation Rate:** attributed first opens reaching a current, complete Personal Plan within seven days.
2. **Eligible Action Completion Rate:** eligible stop or switch recommendations with required external steps confirmed, divided by eligible recommendations viewed.
3. **Confirmed Estimated Saving per Eligible User:** median net amount across matured avoided-renewal observations, including eligible users with zero.

### Funnel diagnostics

- Proof-module view and assumptions-open rate.
- Landing-to-App-Store click rate by CTA position.
- Attributed first-open rate.
- Account and portfolio completion rate.
- Eligible savings-opportunity incidence.
- Recommendation view-to-start and start-to-confirm rates.
- Renewal-outcome response rate.
- Android and general email conversion.

### Guardrails

- Partial, stale, or failed Plan exposure rate.
- Currency claims backed by current recommendation-grade evidence.
- Cancellation confirmed but later reported charged.
- Recommendation reversal or dismissal.
- Duplicate or missing events across clients and backend.
- Support, complaint, refund, or trust issues associated with the claim.

### Minimum event vocabulary

Acquisition:

- `marketing_page_view`
- `proof_module_view`
- `proof_assumptions_opened`
- `app_store_cta_clicked`
- `android_waitlist_started`
- `android_waitlist_submitted`

Activation:

- `first_open_attributed`
- `account_created`
- `first_title_added`
- `subscription_portfolio_completed`
- `personal_plan_became_current`
- `eligible_savings_opportunity_generated`

Outcome:

- `savings_recommendation_viewed`
- `savings_recommendation_action_started`
- `provider_handoff_opened`
- `external_completion_confirmed`
- `cancellation_became_effective`
- `expected_renewal_date_elapsed`
- `renewal_outcome_reported`
- `subscription_restarted`
- `savings_observation_window_closed`

Client analytics explain behaviour. Server-side frozen facts support financial claims. Do not reconstruct a public savings claim from click telemetry.

## 10. Experiment sequence

Run two sequential experiments. Do not run a multivariate test before baseline volume is known.

### Experiment 1: mechanism

- **Control:** Current mechanism headline after unsupported figures are removed.
- **Treatment:** “Stop paying for the wait.”
- Keep proof, visuals, CTA, and placement identical.
- Primary metric: App Store CTA click-through.
- Secondary metric: qualified Plan activation within seven days.

### Experiment 2: proof

- **Control:** Winner from experiment 1 without numeric proof.
- **Treatment:** Winner plus the dated £19.98 worked example and visible assumptions.
- Primary metric: qualified Plan activation.
- Secondary metrics: CTA click-through and eligible-opportunity incidence.
- Trust guardrails: complaints, discrepancy reports, early churn, and qualification engagement.

Pre-register eligibility, primary metric, minimum detectable effect, sample-size calculation, and analysis date after baseline traffic is measured. If traffic cannot support a powered test, report directional results and interviews instead of claiming statistical significance.

## 11. Savings evidence programme

Campaign analytics and claim evidence are separate systems. Create an append-only server-side observation for every recommendation used in substantiation.

### Freeze at projection time

- Claim-definition and eligibility version.
- Pseudonymous user, plan, recommendation, workflow, and provider-offer identifiers.
- Region, tier, currency, billing interval, tax treatment, and price evidence.
- Release schedule and availability revisions.
- Baseline and alternative strategies.
- Expected billing dates and other wanted content considered.
- Projected avoided charges and amount.
- Every exclusion and qualification.

### Append as outcomes mature

- Recommendation view, start, handoff, and confirmation timestamps.
- Effective access-end date.
- Each expected renewal date and its charge outcome.
- Evidence method: user attestation, receipt, or financial import.
- Actual charge, restart date, restart cost, and substitute costs.
- Observation close date, net estimated saving, zero-saving reason, and completeness.

Use this calculation:

```text
confirmed estimated saving
  = matured baseline charges confirmed not charged
  - incremental or earlier restart charges caused by the alternative plan
```

Merge overlapping title windows at the billable-product level. Include zero outcomes. A cancellation confirmation alone does not prove that a renewal was avoided.

## 12. Ninety-day evidence roadmap

### Days 0 to 14

- Remove or quarantine unsupported quantitative and social-proof claims.
- Approve claim definitions, event contract, privacy rules, and experiment plan.
- Instrument landing attribution, both clients, and the server evidence seam.
- Start the prospective annual cohort clock.

### Days 15 to 30

- Launch the mechanism experiment.
- Add billing-date outcome follow-up.
- Audit attribution joins, duplicates, Plan completeness, and price freshness.
- Publish no aggregate savings claim.

### Days 31 to 60

- Analyze experiment 1 at its fixed stopping point.
- Launch the worked-example experiment.
- Mature the first avoided-renewal observations.
- Manually reconcile a sample from recommendation through billing outcome.

### Days 61 to 90

- Close a predefined 90-day cohort.
- Publish a 90-day result only if sample size, evidence completeness, and advertising review pass.
- Freeze the annual cohort definition and continue observation.

A defensible annual claim cannot exist after 90 days. The first annual aggregate requires the earliest predefined cohort to complete a full 12 months, with zero-savers included and the analysis independently reproduced.

## 14. Budget options

### Lean organic

- Founder-led content and community.
- Four reusable templates.
- Existing HubSpot and site hosting.
- Engineering focused on landing, calculator, and measurement.
- No paid media until attribution passes.

### Validated paid pilot

- Add £140 to £280 for a 14-day, £10 to £20 daily media test.
- Put all spend behind the best organic creative.
- Stop if qualified activation, evidence quality, or trust guardrails deteriorate.

### Growth

- Creator retainers, broader creative production, search/editorial expansion, and larger media tests only after one channel repeatedly produces qualified activation.

## 15. Launch gates

1. **Claim hygiene:** Every quantitative claim has evidence, visible qualifications, an owner, and a refresh date.
2. **Product truth:** The marketed Plan behaviour is reachable and verified in the active iOS runtime.
3. **Landing readiness:** Page, calculator, mobile layout, source links, consent, CTAs, attribution, staging, and production deployment all pass.
4. **Measurement parity:** iOS and Android event contracts align, production transports work, and server facts reconcile.
5. **Evidence readiness:** Price and schedule facts are frozen, partial and ineligible cases fail closed, and calculations reproduce.
6. **Experiment readiness:** Baseline exists, assignment persists, metrics and stopping rules are fixed, and install attribution is tested.
7. **Distribution readiness:** App Store copy is live and verified. Android remains waitlist-only until its intended public track is proven.
8. **Scale gate:** Paid spend begins only after qualified activation and trust guardrails are acceptable.

## 16. Definition of campaign completion

The campaign is complete when:

- The mechanism, proof module, calculator, and store experience tell one coherent story.
- Six weeks of content have shipped through the selected channels.
- Campaign attribution reaches a current, complete Personal Plan.
- The campaign report separates reach, clicks, installs, qualified activation, projected savings, matured outcomes, and unknown outcomes.
- Every numeric public claim can be reproduced from retained evidence.
- A clear scale, iterate, or stop decision has been made for each channel.
- The prospective 12-month cohort continues without being misrepresented as annual evidence.
